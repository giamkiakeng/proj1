Certificate for the germs of complexity <= 15 refuted by the lines 2..10 (README, Section 12)
=============================================================================================

The same construction as results/germs/CERTIFICATES.txt of the paper's supplement (Proposition 6.4): the
completion test of germinc.py for the lengths 2..10 (half-line to anti-diagonal 78, pumping pairs to 39, no
symmetry breaking), one formula with a selector variable per germ (src/germcert.py of the supplement).  The formula
is unsatisfiable iff every germ of the list is refuted.

1500 germs (the UNSATISFIABLE lines of K15_n10.log.gz) = K15_refuted10.txt.gz; together with the 135 germs of
K15_surv10.txt they are exactly the 1635 germs of germs_K15.txt.gz.
  zcat K15_refuted10.txt.gz > K15_refuted10.txt
  python3 ../../../paper/supplementary/src/germcert.py K15_refuted10.txt 10 k15_n10
