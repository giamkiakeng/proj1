\section*{Data availability}
All programs, formula generators, rule tables, germ lists and experiment
logs are publicly available in the repository
\url{https://github.com/giamkiakeng/proj1}, directory \texttt{fssp5}. The
LRAT certificates are not stored (the one of \cref{thm:four} has
$177$\,MB); they are regenerated deterministically by the commands of
\ref{app:repro} and of the file \path{results/germs/CERTIFICATES.txt},
which list their SHA-256 digests.
