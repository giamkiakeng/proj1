\section*{Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the
writing process}
During the preparation of this work the authors used Claude (Anthropic), an
AI assistant, to write and run the programs, to carry out the computer
experiments and to draft the manuscript. After using this tool, the authors
reviewed and edited the content as needed and take full responsibility for
the content of the publication.

\section*{Data availability}
All programs, formula generators, rule tables and experiment logs are
available in the accompanying repository (directory \texttt{fssp5/}). The
LRAT certificate of \cref{thm:four} ($177$\,MB) is not stored; it is
regenerated deterministically in under a minute by the commands of
\ref{app:repro}, and its SHA-256 digest is listed there.
