\paragraph{Data availability}
{\sloppy All data behind the computational statements are archived at Zenodo,
\url{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24002680}: the eight unsatisfiable formulas
of \cref{thm:four,prop:germs,prop:balzer}; their LRAT proofs, $4.4$\,GB in
total, and the DRAT proof from which one of them was obtained; the eight
formulas of the second encoder of \cref{sec:method}; the SHA-256 digests of the
formulas and the proofs; the partial rules and the models behind
\cref{prop:sharp}; the data of the figures; the logs of the runs that
regenerated the proofs and reran every other computation; and this version of
the supplementary
material, which contains the programs, the rule tables, the germ lists and the

computation, the solver times and proof sizes, and the program that draws the
figures. The supplementary material is also in the public repository
\url{https://github.com/giamkiakeng/proj1}, directory
\texttt{fssp5/paper/supplementary}; the version described in this paper is
commit \texttt{7f05ea7}, with the permanent link
\url{https://github.com/giamkiakeng/proj1/tree/7f05ea7c3d67b62723a4d53cfbeb131d1fe46aab/fssp5/paper/supplementary}.

recorded digests (\cref{sec:method,app:artefacts}).\par}
